# Privacy policy — Locked Site Blocker

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

Locked Site Blocker is a Chrome extension that blocks websites you choose. In
short: everything it knows stays in your browser, and nothing is collected or
shared.

## What the extension stores

- The list of websites you block and, for each one, when you added it and when
  its lock ends.
- Whether you've dismissed the Incognito notice in the extension's popup.

This is kept locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage. It isn't
synced to your Google account or to other devices.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no servers and
  makes no network requests of its own.
- It doesn't read, record or change the content of the pages you visit, and it
  doesn't keep a history of your browsing.
- It has no account, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't run remote code.

## Permissions it uses

- **Blocking websites** (`declarativeNetRequest`): tells Chrome which sites on
  your list to block.
- **Storage** (`storage`): keeps your block list on your device.
- **Access to all websites** (`<all_urls>`): lets Chrome show the extension's
  countdown page in place of a site on your list, and reload tabs already open
  on a site when you block it. It covers every site because you decide which
  sites to block.

## Deleting your data

Removing a site from the list (possible once its lock has run out) deletes it.
Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data from your browser.
